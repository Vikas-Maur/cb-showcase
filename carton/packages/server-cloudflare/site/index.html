Every version of your edit, and your team on one timeline

Carton keeps every version of a Cardboard project, shows who changed what, down to the agent, and lets your team edit one project together, live.

Download for Windows View on GitHub

Share a project

  1. Open the project in Cardboard and click the Share icon in the title bar.
  2. Enter this site's address and your name, and press Share.
  3. Under Invite to edit, choose what they can do and send them the link.

You've been invited to a project

Carton runs inside the Cardboard app. Open your invite there to edit together, live.

Open it in Cardboard

  1. Open Cardboard and any project.
  2. Click the Share icon in the title bar.
  3. Paste this link under Join a shared project, add your name, and press Join.
